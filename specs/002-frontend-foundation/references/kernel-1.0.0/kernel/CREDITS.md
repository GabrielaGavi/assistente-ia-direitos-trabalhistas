# Credits

## Images

- The three photos in `assets/imagery/` (`01-space-hero.webp`, `02-material.webp`, `03-object-detail.webp`) were created for this design system as placeholders, generated with AI by HorizonX.
- The product name used in the templates (Ferrule), its customers, prices and metrics are fictional. Issue titles and IDs are sample content.
- Replace the images with your own photography or product screenshots before you publish if your project needs them.

## Fonts

- The styles expect Mona Sans and Fragment Mono, loaded from Google Fonts by `styles.css`. Both are open-source families; check each family's license on Google Fonts before you self-host them. See the Fonts note in `README.md`.

- None. The kit is plain HTML and CSS.
