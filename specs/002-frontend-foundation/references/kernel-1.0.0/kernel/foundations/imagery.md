# Imagery

Dark, cold, precise photographs of the machines behind software, each with one lime point of light that echoes the accent. No people, no logos, no screens with readable text. Product screenshots are built in HTML with `.kn-window`, so they stay crisp and editable.

| File | Role | Subject | Crop and placement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/imagery/01-space-hero.webp` | Space | Long server aisle at night, black racks, white status lights, one lime light | Homepage photo band or deck cover. Symmetrical: put copy in a Void panel on the left third. |
| `assets/imagery/02-material.webp` | Material | Black circuit board macro with copper traces and one lime LED | Reliability or deploy section, 4:3 or 16:9. The left side is dark negative space for a headline. |
| `assets/imagery/03-object-detail.webp` | Object | Black mechanical keyboard with a single lime keycap | Call-to-action band, changelog header or media card. Keep the lime key right of center. |

All three are 1920 x 1080 WebP.

## Rules

- One lime light per photo at most, and only where the frame already has it.
- Text on photos sits inside a solid Void panel (`rgba(8, 9, 10, 0.78)` with a hairline), never on a gradient scrim.
- Photos use `--radius-surface` and the Hairline ring like cards.
- Replacement photos should stay near-black, low-saturation and unbranded. Product UI belongs in an HTML window, not in a photo.
